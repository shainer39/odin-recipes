Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are a simple, fluffy breakfast staple made from a basic batter of flour, sugar, milk, eggs, and butter.

Cooked in a pan until golden on each side, they're soft and light on the inside with a slightly crisp edge.

They're versatile and delicious served with syrup, fresh fruit, butter, or any topping you like

Ingredients

  • flour
  • sugar
  • baking powder
  • pinch of salt
  • milk
  • eggs
  • melted butter

Steps

  1. Mix flour,sugar,baking powder,and a pinch of salt in a bowl
  2. In another bowl, whisk milk,egg, and melted butter
  3. Combine wet and dry ingredients,stir until just mixed
  4. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat
  5. Pour batter to form pancakes,cook until bubbles form on top
  6. Flip,cook other side until golden
  7. Serve warm with syrup,butter,or other toppings
